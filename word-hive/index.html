How to play

Make as many words as you can from the seven letters in the hive.

  • Words need at least 4 letters.
  • Every word must use the gold centre letter.
  • Letters can be used more than once.

Scoring: 4-letter words are worth 1 point; longer words score 1 point per letter. Almost every puzzle has a hive word that uses all seven letters, worth 7 extra points.

Rare words that aren't on the main list still count as bonus words for extra points.

A new hive appears every day, and you can play earlier ones with the arrows.
