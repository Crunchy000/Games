Your move
  • Goal: leave only two colours (or none) in as few taps as you can.
  • Tap a gem to remove it. Press and hold to preview what will clear.
  • A gem is in balance when it and the gems touching it hold equal red, green and blue. It clears, taking those touching gems with it.
  • Gems slide into gaps when that brings their neighbourhood closer to white, and broken-off clusters drift back to the main formation. Both can set off chains.
