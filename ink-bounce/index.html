Ink Bounce

✏️

Draw your paddle

There’s no paddle: you draw it. Draw a line under the ball and it bounces off, then the line is used up. One line at a time.

🖋️

Save your ink

Ink is limited, so short, neat lines go further, score a bonus and refund a little ink. Every brick you hit gives some ink back too.

💧

Special bricks

Bricks with a drop refill lots, bombs blast, and two-circle bricks split the ball. Catch falling ink drops; a gold pot fills you up.
