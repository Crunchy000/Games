Ink Bounce

There's no paddle. You draw it.

  • Draw a line under the ball with your finger. It bounces off, then the line is used up. Only one line at a time: a new one wipes the old.
  • Ink is limited, so short, neat lines go further, score a precision bonus and refund a little ink.
  • Cyan bricks refill ink, red ones blast, yellow ones split the ball. Catch falling ink drops with your line or the ball; a gold pot fills you up.
