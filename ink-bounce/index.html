Ink Bounce

There's no paddle. You draw it.

  • Draw a line under the ball with your finger. It bounces off, then the line is used up.
  • Ink is limited and refills slowly, so short, neat lines go further and score a precision bonus.
  • Break every brick. Blue bricks refill ink, red ones explode, gold ones split the ball.
