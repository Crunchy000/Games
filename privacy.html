Privacy at The Bear & Bean Games

The short version: we don't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and nothing to buy.

What the games keep, and where

Your scores, progress in a game and settings (such as sound on or off, light or dark, and your favourite games) are saved only on your own device, in its browser storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser or phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

What we don't do

  • No sign-ups, logins or accounts.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics code of any kind.
  • No cookies used to track you, and no sharing or selling of data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

How the games reach you

The games are ordinary web pages. They are hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which, like any web host, handle your device's network address to send the pages to you and to keep the service secure. We do not receive or keep that information. Once loaded, the games work offline.

The Android app

The Bear & Bean Games app opens these same pages full screen, so everything above applies to the app too. It asks for no special permissions.

Children

The games are made to be friendly for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before anything new is added.
