Find the butterfly

Every butterfly here has its own wings. Bear and Bean are looking for one special butterfly: find it among the flowers.

👀

Check both

The right one has the same wing colour and the same pattern. Lots of butterflies share one of those, so look closely.

🎨

The patterns

Eyespot, Spotty, Tiger stripes, Veined, Checked, Two-tone, Edged, Hearts and Starry.

👆

Make them fly

Butterflies cluster on knapweed and flowering bushes. Tap by a cluster and they take off, land nearby for a few seconds, then flutter back, so you can see who was hiding. Tap a match to find it. Tapping a butterfly that isn't a match sends them flying too, but costs 5 points.

🔍

Look around

Drag to look around, and pinch or tap ＋ to zoom. Tap the little map to jump. A hint 🔍 costs 25; ladybirds 🐞 among the clusters are a bonus.

🌅

90 butterflies to find

Find every butterfly, one at a time, and watch the day move from morning to golden hour as you find them. Tap ↻ to choose the wildflower meadow or the tropical rainforest.
