Bear and Bean are both on the ice. Take turns tapping a block to knock it out. A block needs two neighbours (the frame counts) and a path to the frame, or it drops too. Sink the other one first, but don't sink yourself!
Instead of knocking, you can tap yourself and hop to a neighbouring block (not on the edge), but not two goes in a row.
