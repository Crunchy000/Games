How to play

🍬

Match three

Swipe a gummy onto a neighbour (or tap one, then the other) to swap them. Line up 3 or more of a kind to pop them.

🚀

Make specials

🚀 4 in a row: a Rocket clears its row or column.
💥 L or T: a Bomb blasts around it.
🌈 5 in a row: a Rainbow Orb pops every gummy of one kind.
✨ Swap two specials for a mega combo!

🍯

Tricky squares

🍯 Drop honey pots out of the bottom for Bear.
🔒 Match a locked gummy once to free it.
🧊 Pop beside sugar cubes to clear them.
🍫 Chocolate spreads unless you clear some.

🍭

Boosters and moves

Every level and every try gives you 3 Lolly Pops (pop any square) and 3 Free Swaps (swap without a match). Spare moves turn into bonus rockets, and once a level you can have 5 more.
