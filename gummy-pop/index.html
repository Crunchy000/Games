How to play

Swipe a gummy onto a neighbour (or tap one, then the other) to swap them. Line up 3 or more of the same kind to pop them.

🚀 4 in a row makes a Rocket that clears its whole row or column.

💥 An L or T shape makes a Bomb that blasts the gummies around it.

🌈 5 in a row makes a Rainbow Orb. Swap it with any gummy to pop every one of that kind.

✨ Swap two specials together for a mega combo!

Finish each level's goal before you run out of moves. Moves left over turn into bonus rockets.
