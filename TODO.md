# Game ideas

Ideas for future games. Each one will be an original take with its own name, characters, art and sounds,
built to the same rules as the rest of the arcade: one self-contained file, touch-first, works offline and
non-violent.

- [x] ~~**Inspired by 2048:**~~ Done as **Bean Merge**.
  Original idea: slide numbered tiles on a 4×4 grid; matching tiles merge and double.
- [x] ~~**Inspired by Subway Surfers:**~~ Done as **River Rush** (Bear on a runaway log).
  Original idea: an endless runner with three lanes; swipe to switch lanes, jump and duck past obstacles and collect coins.
- [ ] **Inspired by Cut the Rope:** a physics puzzle; cut ropes to swing a treat into a hungry creature's mouth, collecting stars on the way.
- [x] ~~**Inspired by Helix Jump:**~~ Covered by **Stack Drop** (hold to smash down a spinning ring tower).
  Original idea: a ball bounces down a spinning tower; drag to turn the tower so the ball drops through the gaps and avoids the coloured segments.
- [ ] **Bear vs Bean: inspired by Hungry Hippos (2 players):** same-screen for two; Bear and Bean sit at opposite ends of the table and tap to gobble rolling snacks, and the most snacks wins.
- [ ] **Bear vs Bean: Four in a Row (2 players):** honey pots against jelly beans.
- [ ] **Bear vs Bean: Coaster Hockey (2 players):** air hockey on a pub table with beer mats.
- [ ] **Nonograms:** picture logic puzzles that reveal little Bear & Bean pictures.
- [ ] **Pub Quiz:** a daily set of ten questions.
