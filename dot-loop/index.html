How to play

🔴

Link the dots

Drag across neighbouring dots of the same colour (up, down, left or right), and let go to clear them. Drag back to undo a link.

⭕

Close a loop

Join back onto your own line to make a loop: it clears every dot of that colour, and dots trapped inside turn into bombs that set each other off.

🌰

Tricky bits

❄️ Frost melts when you clear the dots on it.
🌰 Acorns can’t be linked: drop them out of the bottom.
🌈 Rainbow dots link up with any colour.

🔨

Power-ups

Every level and every try gives you three of each, and they don’t use a move. Tap one, then tap a dot:
🔨 Pop clears that dot.
🎨 Colour clears every dot of its colour.

⏱️

Two ways to play

Levels: collect the dots shown before you run out of moves; spare moves earn stars.

60-second dash: clear all you can. Every loop adds 2 seconds.
