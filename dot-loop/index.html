How to play

Drag across neighbouring dots of the same colour (up, down, left or right) to link them. Let go to clear them.

Drag back over a dot to undo the last link.

Join back onto your own line to close a loop. A loop clears every dot of that colour on the board, and any dots trapped inside it too.

Levels: collect the dots shown before you run out of moves. Moves left over earn stars.

60-second dash: clear as many dots as you can. Every loop adds 2 seconds.
