The Bear & Bean presents

Fishy Floes

🐧

Slide and catch

On your turn, slide a penguin in a straight line as far as you like. It can’t jump gaps or other penguins. The floe it leaves sinks, and you keep its fish.

👆

One tap a turn

Tap a glowing floe to move there. On the big board one of your three penguins is picked for you: tap another of yours to switch.

🏝️

Stuck or alone

A penguin with nowhere to go climbs out with its floe. One with an island all to itself scoops up the most fish it could get there.

🐟

Most fish wins

One penguin each on the small board, three on the big one, starting on fair, random floes. Play a friend or the computer.
