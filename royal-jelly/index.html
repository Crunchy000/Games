How to play

Every hive needs a queen. Place one queen bee in:

  • every row
  • every column
  • every colour patch

And no two queen bees may touch, not even corner to corner.

Tap a square to place a queen bee, and tap her again to take her off. Squares she rules out get dotted for you automatically.

You can also drag across squares to add your own dots as reminders.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer, so you never need to guess.
