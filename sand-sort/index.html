How to play

Pour the sand

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour.

🎨

Same colour only

Sand can only land on the same colour, or go into an empty tube. Fill a tube with one colour and it gets a cork.

❓

Mystery sand

On mystery sand levels the lower sand stays hidden until you uncover it. Stuck? Undo, or add one spare tube each level.
