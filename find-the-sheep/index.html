Find the Sheep

🐑

Find the sheep

Bear and Bean are looking for one special sheep. Find every sheep in the meadow that matches it.

👀

Check all three

A match has the same colour, the same knitted pattern and the same face, black or white. Lots of sheep share one or two of those, so look closely.

🧶

The patterns

Stripy, Zigzag, Argyle diamonds, Spotty, Hearts, Starry, Fair Isle snowflakes, Tartan checks and Patchwork quilt squares.

👆

Shoo and find

The sheep huddle in flocks. Tap a sheep that isn't a match and it trots out of the way (5 points), so you can see who's hiding behind. Tap a match to find it.

🔍

Look around

Drag to look around, and pinch or tap ＋ to zoom. Tap the little map to jump. A hint 🔍 costs 25; four-leaf clovers 🍀 under the flocks are a bonus.

🌙

12 hunts a field

More sheep to find as you go, and the day moves on every three hunts. Tap ↻ for a new field.
