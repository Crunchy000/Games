Sheep Wearing Jumpers

🐑

Find the sheep

The sheep have just been sheared, so they're keeping warm in knitted jumpers. Every sheep wears a different one. Bear and Bean are looking for one special jumper: find the sheep wearing it.

👀

Check both

The right sheep has the same jumper colour and the same knitted pattern. Lots of sheep share one of those, so look closely.

🧶

The patterns

Stripy, Zigzag, Argyle diamonds, Spotty, Hearts, Starry, Fair Isle snowflakes, Tartan checks and Patchwork quilt squares.

👆

Scatter the flock

The sheep huddle in flocks. Tap the grass by a flock and they scatter wide for a few seconds, then slowly huddle back together, so you can see who's hiding in the middle. Tap a match to find it. Tapping a sheep that isn't a match scatters them too, but costs 5 points.

🔍

Look around

Drag to look around, and pinch or tap ＋ to zoom. Tap the little map to jump. A hint 🔍 costs 25; four-leaf clovers 🍀 under the flocks are a bonus.

🌙

63 sheep a field

Find every sheep in the field, one at a time, and watch the day move from morning to starlight as the field empties. Tap ↻ for a new field.
