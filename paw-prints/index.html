How to play

Read the clues

The numbers say how many squares in a row or column are filled, in order. 1 1 means one, a gap, then one more.

✏️

Fill and cross

Tap or drag to fill squares. Switch to cross to mark squares you know are empty.

🖼️

Reveal the picture

Finished lines fill with colour; finish them all to see the picture. It's easy mode: wrong guesses are put right for you.
