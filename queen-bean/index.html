How to play

👑

Crown the queens

Crown one Queen Bean in every row, every column and every colour patch.

No touching

No two Queen Beans may touch, not even corner to corner.

👆

Tap to crown

Tap a square to crown a Queen Bean, and tap her again to take her off. Squares she rules out are dotted for you; drag across squares to add your own dots.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer, so you never need to guess.
